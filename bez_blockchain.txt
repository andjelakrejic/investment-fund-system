PREBACIVANJE NA NO-BLOCKCHAIN VERZIJU I NAZAD

1. PRIPREMA (URADI UNAPRED, DOK IMAS INTERNET - NE NA ODBRANI)

Proveri da folder postoji:

Get-ChildItem .\director-no-blockchain

Treba da vidis:
- director-no-blockchain\Dockerfile
- director-no-blockchain\director\application.py
- director-no-blockchain\director\configuration.py
- director-no-blockchain\director\routes\director.py

Build no-blockchain image-a (iz root-a projekta):

docker build -f .\director-no-blockchain\Dockerfile -t projekat-director-service-nb:latest .

Provera:

docker images

Mora da postoji:

projekat-director-service-nb   latest

Ako radis rezervni .tar za odbranu (kao za ostale image-e), dodaj i ovaj:

docker save -o .\iep-images-nb.tar projekat-director-service-nb:latest


2. PREBACIVANJE NA NO-BLOCKCHAIN

kubectl apply -f .\kubernetes\no-blockchain\director.yaml -n investment-fund
kubectl rollout status deployment/director-service -n investment-fund --timeout=120s
kubectl get pods -n investment-fund

Ovo samo azurira postojeci director-service deployment na novi image
(rolling update). Ne dira authentication, employee, mongo, redis, ganache.


3. PROVERA DA JE NOVI (NO-BLOCKCHAIN) KOD STVARNO AKTIVAN

kubectl exec -n investment-fund deployment/director-service -- ls /app/director

NE SME da postoji blockchain.py ni voting_worker.py u listingu.
Ako ih vidis, pod je i dalje na starom image-u - obrisi ga rucno:

kubectl get pods -n investment-fund
kubectl delete pod -n investment-fund IME_DIRECTOR_PODA

Kubernetes ce napraviti novi pod sa novim image-om.

Ako port-forward terminal za director-service (5002) ispuca posle rollout-a,
samo ga ponovo pokreni:

kubectl port-forward -n investment-fund service/director-service 5002:5002

Ostala tri port-forward terminala (authentication, employee, blockchain)
ne diras.


4. CISCENJE BAZA PRE GRADER RUN-A

Ako si vec ranije testirala blockchain verziju, ocisti podatke pre nego sto
pokrenes grader na no-blockchain verziji (grader je stateful):

kubectl exec -n investment-fund deployment/order-storage -- redis-cli FLUSHALL
kubectl exec -n investment-fund deployment/investment-database -- mongosh investment --eval "db.dropDatabase()"

$MYSQL_PASSWORD = (kubectl exec -n investment-fund deployment/authentication-database -- printenv MYSQL_ROOT_PASSWORD).Trim()
kubectl exec -n investment-fund deployment/authentication-database -- mysql -uroot "-p$MYSQL_PASSWORD" -e "USE authentication; DELETE FROM user WHERE role != 'director';"


5. GRADER BEZ BLOCKCHAINA

cd PUTANJA_DO\iep_testovi
.\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1

pytest -q --type all `
  --authentication-url http://127.0.0.1:5000 `
  --jwt-secret development-secret-key-at-least-32-characters `
  --roles-field role `
  --employee-role employee `
  --director-role director `
  --with-authentication `
  --employee-url http://127.0.0.1:5001 `
  --director-url http://127.0.0.1:5002 `
  --grade-report-file grade_report_no_blockchain.json

Bez --with-blockchain i --provider-url - grader sam prepoznaje no-blockchain
granu i salje {uuid, approved} umesto {uuid, voters}.

Maksimum u ovom modu:
- authentication: 49
- level0: 27
- level1: 26  (jedan poen vise nego u blockchain modu - dodatni content-check
               test koji postoji samo ovde)
- level2: 29
- level3: 49
- TOTAL: 180
- 98 testova

Ocekivani rezultat:
180/180
98 passed


6. POVRATAK NA BLOCKCHAIN VERZIJU

kubectl apply -f .\kubernetes\director.yaml -n investment-fund
kubectl rollout status deployment/director-service -n investment-fund --timeout=120s
kubectl get pods -n investment-fund

Provera da je stari (blockchain) kod ponovo aktivan:

kubectl exec -n investment-fund deployment/director-service -- ls /app/solidity/output

Mora da postoje Voting.abi i Voting.bin.

Ako pre ovoga menjas grader run, ocisti baze ponovo (isti koraci kao u tacki 4)
pre nego sto pokrenes pytest sa --with-blockchain --provider-url http://127.0.0.1:8545.


7. VAZNO

- Ne mesati kubectl apply -f .\kubernetes\ (sve odjednom) sa ovim rucnim
  prebacivanjem - .\kubernetes\no-blockchain\ je namerno IZVAN glavnog foldera
  da ga bulk apply ne pokupi slucajno.
- ganache pod (blockchain deployment) moze mirno da ostane da radi neiskoriscen
  dok je director-service na no-blockchain image-u - ne mora da se gasi.
- Grader sam bira format /decision tela na osnovu prisustva --with-blockchain
  flaga - ne treba nista drugo menjati u komandi osim tog jednog flaga i
  --provider-url.
- Ako /decision vraca 500 posle prebacivanja, prvo:
  kubectl logs -n investment-fund deployment/director-service --tail=100
